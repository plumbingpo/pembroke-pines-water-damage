Where Water Damage Starts in Jacksonville Properties

Starting at the top of a home and working toward the ground is a handy way to think about water damage. That is the route we take when inspecting a property in Jacksonville, and five spots along it account for most calls.

Attics: The top of the house takes on water when shingles are damaged or the seal around a vent pipe gives out. Once soaked, that material no longer does its job and holds moisture against the ceiling joists. We take out the ruined material, dry the framing and leave the roof repair itself to a roofer.

Bathrooms: A tub left filling, a sink with a blocked overflow hole or a toilet that runs over can soak a bathroom floor quickly. Walls: Supply lines and drain pipes run inside the walls, and a slow leak there can go on for a long time.
